## Detailed description

Track your deck's value like a portfolio. DuelTicker pulls live market prices for any Yu-Gi-Oh! card — TCGplayer, eBay, Cardmarket, Amazon, and CoolStuffInc — and shows the numbers that matter: current price, 24-hour change, all-time high/low, and 7-day volatility in a clean toolbar popup that refreshes every 60 seconds.

**What you can do:**

- **Search any card** — type a name like "Blue-Eyes White Dragon" and add it to your watchlist in one click (smart hyphen matching handles the names)
- **Cross-market prices** — TCGplayer, eBay, Cardmarket, Amazon, and CoolStuffInc all on one row
- **Price trends at a glance** — trend arrows from client-side history, 24h change, ATH/ATL range, and 7-day volatility %
- **30-day sparkline** — a mini price chart on every card row, hover for a 7-day breakdown with dates
- **Price alerts** — set alert-above, alert-below, or percentage-based thresholds; fires a chime when crossed
- **24-hour alert log** — review every alert that fired across all your cards
- **Sort & organize** — sort by price, gainers/losers, name, or added order; ⭐ pin favorites to the top; drag to reorder
- **Right-click menu** — copy a price or name, remove a card instantly
- **Keyboard navigation** — Arrow keys to move through cards, Enter to open the listing, Delete to remove
- **Dark/light theme** — toggle between modes, persisted between sessions
- **Export & copy** — download your watchlist as CSV, or copy all prices to clipboard in one click
- **Compact view** — shrink rows to fit more cards on screen
- **Snooze alerts** — mute all alerts for an hour with one click, countdown shown
- **Undo remove** — accidentally deleted a card? Undo link stays for 4 seconds
- **Refresh countdown ring** — see when the next 60s auto-refresh will happen
- **Ctrl/Cmd+R** — instant manual refresh
- **Ctrl/Cmd+K** — jump to search instantly
- **Ctrl/Cmd+Plus/Minus/0** — zoom the popup
- **Offline resilience** — shows your last good prices with a color-coded staleness indicator when the API is down

Data comes from the free, public YGOPRODeck API. No accounts needed, no API keys to paste.

**Private by design.** Your watchlist, favorites, price history, and alert thresholds live entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
