**Category:** Shopping

**Full description**

Your deck is an asset — track it like one. DuelTicker turns any Yu-Gi-Oh! card search into a live price ticker right from your toolbar.

Search any card — Blue-Eyes White Dragon, Dark Magician, whatever you're running — and add it to your watchlist. Each card shows its real TCGplayer market price, with eBay as a fallback, plus a cross-market line (TCGplayer · eBay · Cardmarket · Amazon) so you can see where the deals are. Every card keeps its own price history in your browser, so you get real trend arrows (▲/▼ since your last check) and a mini sparkline of recent moves. A scrolling ticker tape across the top of the popup keeps your whole collection moving in real time, and it auto-refreshes while the popup is open.

Search even handles hyphenated names: type "blue eyes white dragon" and it finds Blue-Eyes White Dragon for you. Click any card to jump straight to its YGOPRODeck page.

It all lives in a small popup — no new tab, no clutter. If the price feed drops, your last prices stay on screen with how old they are, color-coded from green (fresh) to red (long offline). Your watchlist is stored locally, and nothing is tracked.

Free to use forever. If the ticker pays for itself, a coffee is always appreciated. Found something broken? Send a quick note to contactae2000@gmail.com.
